== Description ==

**Display Yelp Business Reviews on Your WordPress Site in Under 2 Minutes — Fast, Local & Built for Conversions.**

**WP Yelp Review Slider** downloads your **Yelp business reviews** (including **reviewer avatars** and **customer-uploaded review photos**) and stores them directly in your WordPress database (`utf8mb4` emoji-ready) on your own server. Instead of slowing down your site with external API calls on every page load, your site serves Yelp reviews locally for instant loading, better Core Web Vitals, and full page-caching support.

Whether you run a restaurant, local service business, salon, auto shop, dental/medical practice, or multi-location company, displaying verified **5-star Yelp reviews**, customer photos, and a **Yelp rating summary badge** builds immediate **social proof** and helps turn website visitors into paying customers.

### Why Choose WP Yelp Review Slider?

* **Multi-Location Yelp Support:** Add multiple Yelp Business Page URLs on the **Get Yelp Reviews** page, download reviews for each location separately, and choose which Yelp business page each template displays.
* **Customer Review Photos & Lightbox:** Automatically download photos attached to Yelp reviews and display them in a clickable full-size lightbox popup.
* **Yelp Summary Rating Badge:** Place a customizable Yelp rating badge on the left, right, or above your reviews (or show the badge standalone) with your business name, photo, average star rating, review count, and *"Review us on Yelp"* button.
* **Built-In Review Analytics Dashboard:** Track your average Yelp rating, 1-to-5 star breakdown, review volume timeline, rating trends over time, and positive/negative keyword word clouds right inside wp-admin.
* **Stored Locally on Your Server:** Fast, reliable local database storage with zero third-party script lag on your front end.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `wp-yelp-review-slider` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install **WP Yelp Review Slider** from **Plugins → Add New** in WordPress.
2. Activate the plugin through the **Plugins** menu in WordPress.
3. Open **WP Yelp** in your WordPress admin menu.
4. Go to **Get Yelp Reviews**, paste your Yelp Business Page URL(s), and click **Download Reviews**.
5. Check **Review List** and **Analytics** to inspect your imported reviews, customer photos, and rating charts.
6. Create a template on the **Templates** page, then insert the **WP Yelp Review Slider** block or add the shortcode to any page, post, or widget area.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Are Yelp reviews stored on my server? =

Yes. When you download reviews from Yelp, they are saved in your WordPress database on your own server. Your site serves those stored reviews locally instead of calling Yelp on every page view, which keeps your pages fast and cache-friendly.

= Can I download reviews from multiple Yelp business pages? =

Yes. On the **Get Yelp Reviews** page you can add multiple Yelp business URLs and download reviews for each location. In the free version, each review template can display reviews for one chosen Yelp location. In [WP Review Slider Pro](https://wpreviewslider.com/), you can also combine multiple locations and 150+ review platforms into a single template.

= Can I display customer photos from Yelp reviews? =

Yes! Customer photos attached to Yelp reviews are downloaded and displayed as thumbnails in your review slider or grid, and open in a clickable lightbox popup.

= Can I add a Yelp rating summary badge? =

Yes. Every template includes a **Badge** tab that lets you display a customizable Yelp summary badge on the left, right, or top of your reviews — or hide the reviews to display a standalone badge.

= Can I hide specific reviews or filter out low-star ratings? =

Yes. You can set a minimum star rating in your template **Filter** settings, or click the visibility icon on the **Review List** page to hide any individual review.

= How do I request a new feature? =

We are always looking for features to add! Post on the support forum or contact us on the [WP Review Slider website](https://wpreviewslider.com/contact/).
